The original requirements were only: share with users in the same workspace; view-only or editable, chosen by the owner when sharing; secure and scalable; errors handled gracefully; owner can revoke at any time, effective immediately; no sharing outside the workspace; comprehensive unit tests; intuitive UI. The PRD adds no requirement beyond these. A short problem statement, and open questions marked as such, are allowed.
